 Prompt 1

Historical Theme: Art and the Afterlife
Historical Period: Ancient Egypt, New Kingdom, 1333–1323 BCE
Inspired by: Funerary Mask of Tutankhamun

Create a frontal close-up of an Ancient Egyptian funerary mask made of solid gold with inlaid blue glass, lapis lazuli, quartz, and red stones. Show a calm, idealized young pharaoh with almond-shaped eyes, dark blue eyebrows, smooth skin, and no visible emotion. Add the blue and gold striped nemes headdress, a cobra and vulture on the forehead, a long false beard, and a broad decorative collar.

Use perfect symmetry, smooth texture, repeated lines, and rich gold and blue color to create balance and emphasis. Place the mask against a dark background so it fills most of the image.

The mask should clearly represent Ancient Egyptian beliefs about the afterlife. It was created to protect the pharaoh’s spirit and help him live forever after death. The gold and precious materials show the ruler’s divine power, wealth, and importance
