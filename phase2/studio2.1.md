Prompt A — Painting (Watercolor)

Create an artwork using watercolor painting that emphasizes the elements and principles of line, color, texture, contrast, and movement. Use soft watercolor brushes, watercolor paper, and diluted pigments to apply the wet-on-wet and layering techniques characteristic of watercolor. The composition should feature a peaceful mountain landscape with a winding river flowing through a forest during sunrise.

Allow the watercolor pigments to blend naturally on wet paper, creating soft gradients and transparent color layers that convey atmospheric depth. The flowing lines of the river should guide the viewer’s eye through the composition, creating movement and unity, while warm sunrise colors contrast with the cool blues and greens of the landscape. The natural bleeding edges, watery textures, and light washes should emphasize the calm mood and harmony of the scene.


Prompt B — Printmaking (Linocut)

Create a linocut print emphasizing the same visual design: a peaceful mountain landscape with a winding river flowing through a forest during sunrise, while highlighting the elements and principles of line, contrast, shape, and unity. Use linoleum carving tools, ink rollers (brayers), and printing paper to produce the image through the relief printing process of carving and inking.

The composition should rely on bold carved lines and simplified shapes to represent the mountains, trees, and river. Strong black and white contrast should define the landscape and guide the viewer’s eye along the river, creating movement throughout the print. The carved textures in the mountains and trees should provide visual rhythm and unity, while the relief printing process emphasizes crisp edges, high contrast, and graphic textures typical of linocut prints.
